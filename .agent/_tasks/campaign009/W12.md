## Mission — broad accessibility audit of the product shell
1. Audit + fix: roles; labels; hints; selected/disabled states; modal
   semantics; focus order; touch target sizes (>=44pt); dynamic text scaling;
   answer leakage via accessibility tree (in YOUR surfaces); color-only
   communication; reduced motion (`use-reduced-motion`); screen-reader result
   feedback on results screen.
2. Prefer shared fixes in your owned components over per-screen patches.
3. Create reusable a11y contract tests for your surfaces.
4. Do not rewrite any game mechanics or navigation structure.

## Validation
- `cd apps/mobile && npx jest src/components src/__tests__ -t "a11y|accessib"` plus your new tests
- `npx tsc --noEmit` → only your files may have errors.
