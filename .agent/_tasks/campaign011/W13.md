### Failure findings (classified)

- **F13-1 · HIGH · reward-loss vector in `QuestRepository.claim` (FIXED).**
  `src/db/quests.ts` gated the claim UPDATE only on `claimed_at IS NULL`, so any
  stale/direct caller could burn the claim marker of a NEVER-COMPLETED quest
  row — when the quest is legitimately completed later, `applyQuestReward`
  finds `claimedAt !== null` and reports `already-claimed`: that period's
  reward is permanently lost (violates the packet invariant "no reward loss").
  Symptom: adversarial test "quest completion regresses between collect and
  claim" — `db.quests.claim()` returned `true` for an incomplete row. Root
  cause: missing completion precondition at the repository boundary. Fix: claim
  UPDATE now also requires `completed_at IS NOT NULL`; docstring updated.
  Defense-in-depth verified: service layer re-checks progress ≥ goal
  (`QuestNotCompleteError`) at claim time. Evidence: pinned in
  `src/db/__tests__/quests.test.ts` (uncompleted-row claim → false, marker
  stays null) and `src/rewards/__tests__/claim-all-attacks.test.ts`
  ("refuses honestly…" recovery path claims exactly once after re-completion).
  Test-fixture triage note (contract rule 4): the pre-existing independence
  assertion (`db/__tests__/quests.test.ts` ~line 125) used an UNCOMPLETED row
  as its fixture for "different periods claim independently"; its intent is
  valid but its fixture relied on the defect. Fixture corrected to a completed
  row; intent preserved; no assertion weakened.

- **F13-2 · LOW · real-timer race in celebration dismissal test (FIXED).**
  `src/rewards/__tests__/celebration.test.tsx` asserted auto-dismissal after a
  fixed 3600ms sleep against the component's 3500ms timer (~90ms margin);
  failed once under full-swarm CPU load because the dismissal callback itself
  was starved past the assertion. Converted to a condition-based `waitFor`
  (8s timeout): the banner MUST disappear once the real timer fires or the
  test fails — strictly stronger than the sleep, no retries/sleeps/skips
  added. Two consecutive full-scope runs green after the change.

- **F13-3 · LOW · observed (NOT W13 surfaces):** transient `tsc` errors in
  other workers' in-flight files during this window —
  `src/db/__tests__/workout-v2.test.ts` (W07; resolved during my run) and
  `src/analytics/__tests__/projections-differential.test.ts:801` (W10).
  Zero typecheck errors on all W13-owned files. NEEDS_PARENT: none.
