# frontier-product-experience Specification

## Purpose

Consolidated current requirements for `frontier-product-experience`. Generated from the
normative delta specification of `077-frontier-product-experience-rebuild`; behavioral rationale and
design context live in that change's proposal/design documents.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Evidence-driven complete product audit

The campaign MUST assess every player-facing route and supporting state, including scrolling, keyboard, modal, first-use, empty, loading, error, recovery, workout, and data-ownership experiences. Each surface MUST have a KEEP, REFINE, REDESIGN, or REBUILD decision supported by concrete visual, interaction, product, and technical findings.

#### Scenario: A route works but communicates poorly

- **WHEN** a screen renders without exceptions but obscures its primary action or meaningful information
- **THEN** its product-quality finding remains open until the composition and interaction are corrected and inspected on the emulator

### Requirement: One researched art direction

The campaign MUST use actual Refero styles, mobile screens, and flows, compare at least three meaningfully different directions on representative screens, document adopted and rejected patterns, and lock one coherent visual language before broad visual implementation.

#### Scenario: References disagree

- **WHEN** candidate references imply different canvas, density, imagery, or interaction treatments
- **THEN** the selected direction preserves one dominant foundation and documents only bounded secondary contributions rather than averaging unrelated styles

### Requirement: Original offline visual assets and consistent functional icons

The app MUST bundle original generated artwork where it improves comprehension or identity. Accepted ImageGen assets MUST be inspected, have recorded prompts and provenance, verified dimensions/alpha/crops, optimized file size, deterministic local mappings, and React Native emulator evidence in applicable themes and layouts. Functional icons MUST use one crisp coherent vector family and MUST NOT depend on remote services.

#### Scenario: Artwork appears in a game introduction

- **WHEN** an illustration is rendered at a compact size or in dark mode
- **THEN** it remains clear and correctly scaled without obscuring instructions, choices, symbols, timers, or touch targets

### Requirement: Complete shared and major-surface redesign

The app MUST use a consistent token and primitive system for typography, semantic colors, spacing, surfaces, controls, headers, navigation, feedback, game stages/HUD, results, and motion. Home, Games/search/filter/favorites, introductions/tutorials, active gameplay, results/workout completion, Progress/domain/game/activity, Rewards/quests/achievements, Profile/settings, data management/backup/recovery, and supporting states MUST be brought into the selected direction according to the audit.

#### Scenario: Player starts today's training

- **WHEN** Home loads with persisted local progress
- **THEN** today's next action is immediately understandable, recommendations are discoverable, and secondary information does not overwhelm the primary action

### Requirement: Purposeful accessible interactions

Motion MUST reinforce navigation, presses, start, answer feedback, progress, rewards, level updates, and completion while honoring reduced motion. Reaction-time measurement MUST remain independent of decorative animation. Large text, contrast, labeling, color-independent feedback, and platform touch targets MUST remain usable.

#### Scenario: Enlarged text or reduced motion is selected

- **WHEN** the player navigates, plays, receives feedback, or continues a workout
- **THEN** essential actions remain reachable, text does not overlap or clip, and reduced animation does not change measured gameplay performance
