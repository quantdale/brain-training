# Campaign 040 — Release-Candidate Integration & Certification

## Problem

The product has accumulated several validated redesign packets, but readiness
cannot be inferred from their individual reports. The integrated candidate
needs one current, broad, evidence-backed pass across navigation, training,
progression, persistence, accessibility tooling, offline operation, and the
42-game catalog.

## Outcome

Re-observe the shipped experience on the dedicated normal-phone Android
runtime, run the broadest practical repository gates, review unsupported copy
and redesign debt, and classify readiness as certified, conditional, partial,
or blocked. Keep unavailable human, iOS, physical-device, signing, document-
sheet, and external-CI evidence explicit.

## Non-goals

No new feature wave, broad schema/economy change, game-mechanics rewrite,
router rewrite, speculative performance optimization, CI-workflow edit to hide
external failures, or unsupported cognitive/medical claim.
