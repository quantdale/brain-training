## Remaining limits

Odd One Out's sideways triangle can resemble a play symbol; Flexibility's trial-versus-block timing cannot be fully explained in static art; Cue Keeper's image cannot teach disappearance of the watchlist; Grid Recall's secondary echo is small; generated Reaction Time has richer dimensionality than vector-derived covers. These are bounded limitations, not claims that players can infer every rule without instructions. Native typography/theme/scroll inspection is separately recorded.
