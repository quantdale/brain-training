## Primary reference — Duolingo (Playful Green Arcade)

- Energy comes from ONE saturated brand hue + white canvas + rounded display
  type; secondary actions are outlined in a lighter tone of the same family.
- Buttons: filled pill/full-width, bold white label, tactile press; text
  hierarchy is heavy display vs compact body.
- Type: rounded display for headlines only; body stays plain and legible.
- Spacing: comfortable, generous section gaps; illustration carries the
  personality instead of ornament.
- Take: tactile button lip, rounded display headlines, one loud primary hue,
  playful marks; reject: copying the green identity or the illustration style.

## Identity synthesis — "Neon Arcade"

- **Warm paper shell** (cream `#FFF8EF`) + **deep ink play boards**
  (`#0E1020`): the shell is sunny and gamified; the play surface is a focus
  board where stimuli and verdicts pop (Brilliant/LookUp lesson).
- **Vermillion primary** (not Duolingo green) + **volt reward** + **violet
  progression**: a distinct tri-hue system that maps to action / reward /
  progression instead of one brand colour everywhere.
- **Eight domain hues** give every category an identity (orange attention,
  violet flexibility, sky language, teal logic, blue math, pink memory, green
  spatial, yellow speed).
- **Celebration beats**, confetti confined to margins, streak day-dot strip,
  badge gallery with three distinct state treatments.
- **Code-native marks** (spark, flame, blob faces) instead of imported art.

## Patterns to apply per surface (from the 024 briefs, re-used deliberately)

- Home: hero metric inside a progress visual; one dual-line primary CTA;
  streak as day dots + count pill.
- Games: recommendation card with eyebrow pill; category rows with identity
  icons.
- Progress: weekly card anatomy (label → range → hero numeral → 7-bar chart);
  most/least insight pair; dot-matrix calendar.
- Profile/Rewards: 2×2 stat grid with one accent hero card; badge gallery with
  locked desaturation; claimable vs in-progress vs locked treatments.
- Results: celebration → headline → 3 equal metric columns → one wide CTA.
- Play: HUD = exit left, segmented progress centre, pause right; feedback
  sheet or full-bg dye with the prompt restated; speed rounds dye the board.

## Explicit anti-patterns to kill (owner's complaint)

Equal-weight white card stacks; no hero metric; unlabelled thin bars; streak
as text; one treatment for claimable/in-progress/locked; generic empty states;
tint-only tab active state; decorative colour with no identity mapping;
verdict by text colour alone.
