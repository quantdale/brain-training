# Campaign 038 — Accessibility, Device, Motion & Sensory Hardening

## Problem

Campaign 037's clean normal-font matrix is route-verified and automated-a11y
clean, but the audit still reports two interactive nodes clipped by the
tab-hosted content viewport: a Profile Shield purchase control and a Games
catalog card. Large text, alternate phone sizes, reduced motion, and disabled
sound/haptics have not yet been observed on the current implementation.

## Outcome

Establish truthful evidence for high-value journeys under the supported Android
conditions that are available in the dedicated disposable AVD. Repair only
demonstrated reachability or semantics defects, with particular care around
scroll content, the native tab bar, scaled text, and sensory controls.

## Non-goals

No new accessibility framework, router rewrite, game-mechanics change, schema
or persistence change, economy change, unsupported health claim, forced
permission, or claim of manual TalkBack/VoiceOver/iOS/physical-device success
without direct execution.
