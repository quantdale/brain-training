# Campaign 033 — Progress summary and progressive disclosure

## Why

The current Progress route exposes valuable analytics, but the first populated
viewport is dominated by the composite rating and does not answer consistency,
recorded movement, or a next consideration before the deeper history stack.
The sparse state also presents the initial rating too prominently.

## Outcome

Make Progress answer-first while retaining its analytical depth: show
selected-window consistency, cautious sample-aware recorded movement, and one
plain-language domain consideration before the existing composite and
drill-downs. Keep ordinary Game Detail and Progress history routes intact and
make sparse states explicit.

## Scope guard

This is a presentation/disclosure change over existing analytics seams. It does
not alter SQLite schema, session persistence, scoring, rating/mastery formulas,
generators, game mechanics, economy, backup format, or offline behavior.
