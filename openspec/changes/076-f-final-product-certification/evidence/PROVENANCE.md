# Evidence provenance — 076-f terminal certification

**Change:** `076-f-final-product-certification`
**Task:** 1.2 — game/route provenance table
**Companion:** [`SOURCE_EQUIVALENCE.md`](SOURCE_EQUIVALENCE.md) (task 1.3)
**Rows:** [`provenance-table.md`](provenance-table.md) / [`provenance-table.json`](provenance-table.json)

## Why this file exists

Campaign 076 failed once on exactly this point: historical screenshots were
being read as proof about an APK that did not produce them. The rule this file
enforces is the one in the change spec:

> A frame SHALL NOT be labeled as a capture from an APK that did not produce it.

So every frame used for acceptance carries a provenance record with a source
commit, an APK identity, its production dependency, its capture identity, its
visual-review status, its current applicability, and whether recapture is
required.

## The rows are derived, not transcribed

`provenance-table.md` is generated by
`node scripts/certification/build-provenance.mjs` directly from the committed
capture manifests:

| Source manifest | What it supplies |
| --- | --- |
| `…/076-product-wide-ui-ux-reboot/evidence/final-matrix-de6c5fcd/captures.json` | 90 route pairs (3 profiles × 2 themes × 15 surfaces) |
| `…/evidence/final-scroll-de6c5fcd/captures.json` | 2 Results-scroll reachability pairs |
| `…/evidence/after-captures-games/index.json` | 194 retained + 16 quarantined game frames |

The generator recomputes the rendering-dependency diff with `git diff` and
refuses to hand-wave it, so a later production edit cannot quietly leave a
stale "unchanged" claim in place. Re-run it after any source change.

Totals: **302 rows** — 90 route, 2 route-scroll, 194 game, 16 rejected.

## Classification vocabulary

Exactly the three classes the design allows, plus the recapture fields:

| Class | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `CURRENT_APK` | The frame was produced by the terminal APK itself. |
| `SOURCE_EQUIVALENT_HISTORICAL` | Produced by an earlier build whose rendering-dependency closure is unchanged since capture. **Not** a terminal-APK capture — it is comparison evidence only. |
| `NOT_APPLICABLE` | Quarantined/rejected capture, retained for audit and never used for acceptance. |

## How each evidence family is classified

### Route matrix (90 pairs) — `CURRENT_APK`

Every one of the 90 rows binds to `de6c5fcd…` / `c324960…` in the manifest
itself: `apkSha256`, `codeSha`, `pngSha256`, `xmlSha256`, `windowsXmlSha256`,
`visualReview`, `captureIssues`, `routeVerified`, `foregroundVerified`, `blank`.
These were captured from the terminal APK and reviewed as six labelled contact
sheets. Two Results-scroll pairs are likewise `CURRENT_APK`.

The `home` and `progress-detail` surfaces in this matrix are the *only* route
frames that represent the two production edits made after `4a6fc53`, and they
were captured after those edits — see SOURCE_EQUIVALENCE.md.

### Game frames (194 retained) — `SOURCE_EQUIVALENT_HISTORICAL`

Of the 194 retained game frames:

- **22 closure frames** carry a real per-file APK binding to `b2913bca…` at
  source `4a6fc53…` (3 active, 15 feedback, 4 pause — the exact gap states
  listed in the parent `GAME_ASSESSMENT.md` closure table).
- **172 earlier frames** are mixed-build captures with no per-file APK binding.
  They were individually state-verified in the earlier audit and are labelled
  historical.

**None of these is a terminal-APK capture.** The generator writes
`SOURCE_EQUIVALENT_HISTORICAL` for all of them and records the source as
`mixed historical builds (no per-file binding)` where no binding exists, rather
than inventing one. Applicability is `yes (as historical source-equivalent
comparison)`: they may support comparison, and per the spec they **must not**
replace the required current-device check.

### Quarantined frames (16) — `NOT_APPLICABLE`

Retained in `after-captures-games/rejected/` with explicit reasons. Never used
for acceptance.

## Current-device rows are separate again

The per-game rows under
[`current-device/`](current-device/) are the *current-device acceptance checks*
performed during this change against the installed terminal APK. They are a
different evidence family from the historical frames above and do not inherit
their status. A historical frame is never promoted into a current-device row.

## What this file does not claim

- It does not certify any game. That is the current-device assessment, and it
  is recorded separately with its own PASS / FIXED / NOT VALIDATED / N/A value
  per game.
- It does not make the historical frames terminal-APK captures.
- It does not assert release acceptance. Provenance is a precondition for
  acceptance, not acceptance.
