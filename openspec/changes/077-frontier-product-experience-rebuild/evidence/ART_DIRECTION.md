## Locked traits and roles

- Primary foundation: Quizlet's cool#F6F7FB canvas, compact8dp rhythm, clear humanist sans hierarchy, restrained white surfaces, varied colored learning materials. No warm cream/red-studio retreat.
- Bounded Headspace contribution: blue#0061EF for primary interaction, active navigation and selection. Blue is not a decorative page wash. No copied proprietary font/mascot or imported warm whole palette.
- Type: open licensed bundled humanist rounded sans; regular readable body, semibold labels, bold headings. Reduce all-caps/tracking and oversized800-weight headings. Numerals remain tabular; board typography remains mechanic-specific.
- Palette: cool nearwhite/navy-ink light mode; deep cool navy/clear raised surfaces dark mode. Existing semantic success/warning/danger and eight domain roles remain legible and distinct, with explicit on-fill text tokens and measured contrast.
- Surfaces:8dp content-card radius, understated border/elevation, pill primary action, simple flat rows for records/settings. A card must express a meaningful grouping; metrics do not each need floating boxes. 16dp page gutter and responsive layouts, narrower game-stage nesting.
- Media: original sculptural learning-tool silhouettes in each domain's established palette, restrained matte material, precise geometry, soft controlled light, transparent/crop-safe framing. A small Home assembly of learning objects is a companion to the daily action. Title/text always native and outside raster artwork. Game-specific vector/board geometry differentiates siblings; repeating a generic cube is insufficient.
- Functional icons: one coherent crisp vector vocabulary,16–24dp visual size inside48dp Android/44pt iOS touch targets. No mixed text glyphs/emoji for controls. Illustrations never provide essential instructions or intercept input.
- Concrete foundation: embedded Manrope400/600/700 and Lucide20dp/stroke2 with SDK-compatible SVG. Four ExpoRouter bundledTabs retain route/test/back/safe-area contracts and expose that same family on each platform. Launcher/splash use the original interlocking puzzle mark; Play Lab remains an internal direction, not a public product rename.
- Navigation: four existing tabs and names; blue interaction emphasis, quieter clear headers, preserved back fallbacks.
- Home: compact habit/progression rail, one current workout action before four-leg plan; completion becomes a compact honest saved summary and useful next action. No huge empty illustration/black slab.
- Discovery: early recommendation/recent/favorites, clear search/category/filter controls, differentiated covers; large text/compact grids reflow to rows and do not truncate essential titles.
- Intro/tutorial: one meaningful art/board example, brief objective, explicit input/difficulty, reachable start. Avoid two redundant full introductions.
- Gameplay: generous neutral stage, exact row/column/hit geometry, readable cues/HUD/time, concise feedback, opaque fair pause. Art stays outside the cognitive task.
- Results: honest band/records plus up to three useful comparable facts, saved reward, reachable continuation. Trophy/celebration requires real qualifying achievement. Weak20% outcomes keep the existing honest Keep training tone. Comparative faster claims require comparable measured evidence.
- Progress: answer-first overall/domain summary, meaningful direct trend/calendar areas, detailed records one tap away. No long stack of identical Report blocks.
- Profile/Rewards/settings/data: distinct identity/collection, purpose-grouped preferences, clear owner data decisions and preview before destructive apply; polished empty/loading/error/retry.
- Motion: short native-friendly press/transition/feedback/progress/reward cues; no long parade, input blocking, moved CTAs, or reaction-clock coupling. Reduced motion changes decoration only.

## Independent critique to enforce during implementation

The baseline's problems are hierarchy as well as artwork: completed Home black slab/art dominates; Games repeats primitive diagrams; Progress delays overall rating; Profile identity is crowded with faint XP; Results wastes space on sparse art/ring. Preserve useful records, calendar, safe back/pause/press behavior and transactional product logic. Generated concepts are state-mismatched (fresh vs baseline completed workout), so they are not before/after acceptance. Real native vertical slices must be critiqued in dark/compact/2× text before expansion. Refero skill cache disappeared in the continuation; reviewer used persisted real references and the already-read methodology rather than claiming a missing skill was available.
