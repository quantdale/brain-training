076-f: record the D3 repair, the measured device proof, and the new terminal identity

Task 4.1 is now closed on real evidence rather than deferred.

**Defect and fix.** The shared `Button` applied `opacity: 0.5` to the whole
control when disabled, compositing both the fill and the label toward the
background — the label of a disabled primary control collapsed to ~1.63:1. The
repair (button.tsx) dims only the fill to `backgroundElement` and moves the
label to `textSecondary`, and separates `loading` out as its own state so a busy
button keeps its enabled fill and spinner. The slot is measured, not chosen:
`textMuted` on the dimmed light fill is 2.35:1, the same failure mode.

**Device proof in both themes** (measure-d3-pixels.mjs decodes the captured PNG
and computes WCAG contrast):

  light  fill #ECEAE5  label #4A4B4E  -> 7.25:1
  dark   fill #17181B  label #B9BABC  -> 9.14:1

Both match the design tokens exactly, which is what rules out any residual
opacity collapse. The harness, the measurement script and the theme captures are
committed so the number is reproducible rather than asserted.
