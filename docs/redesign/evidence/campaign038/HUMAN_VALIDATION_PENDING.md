# Campaign 038 human/platform validation limits

No human tester was available during this autonomous session. The following
remain **NOT VALIDATED**:

- manual TalkBack announcement quality and focus order;
- iOS VoiceOver, iOS Dynamic Type, and iOS safe-area behavior;
- physical Android hardware, OEM font/rendering differences, and haptics
  hardware;
- store/release signing and production-bundle behavior;
- human visual review of every catalog game under every accessibility setting.

The automated evidence is limited to the named Android emulator, its current
API level, the captured themes/display profiles, and the flows listed in the
other Campaign 038 evidence files. These limits do not block the bounded
Android campaign result, but they prevent a global accessibility claim.
