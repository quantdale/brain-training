# Campaign 036 human and platform validation limits

No independent participant or manual screen-reader session was available for
this campaign. No physical Android device, iOS/VoiceOver runtime, large-text
review, reduced-motion review, store-signed build, or Android system
document-picker/share flow was executed.

The evidence that is actually present is repository-owned Jest/TypeScript/lint
validation, disposable Android emulator pixels/XML, emulator-local ADB flow
execution, UIAutomator inspection, an automated accessibility audit, fresh
logcat review, and a local Android debug build/install. None of those results
is being presented as human or iOS validation.

ARTEMIS was not used for Campaign 036; the deterministic local ADB/UIAutomator
path was sufficient for the bounded clean-install and first-play evidence.
