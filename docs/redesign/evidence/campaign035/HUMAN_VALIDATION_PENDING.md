# Campaign 035 human and platform validation limits

No independent human participant or manual screen-reader session was
performed during this campaign. No physical Android device, iOS/VoiceOver
device, large-text review, reduced-motion review, store-signed build, or
system document-picker flow was run.

Repository-owned Jest, TypeScript, lint, native Android emulator captures,
UIAutomator inspection, and the automated accessibility audit are recorded as
actual evidence. They must not be presented as human or iOS validation.

No ARTEMIS or computer-use journey was needed for this bounded visual slice;
the deterministic ADB/UIAutomator capture path was stable after the documented
cold lazy-load warm-up. ARTEMIS journeys from Campaigns 033 and 034 remain
recorded in their own evidence packages and are not reused as Campaign 035
evidence.
