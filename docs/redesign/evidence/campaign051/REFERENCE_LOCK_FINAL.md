# Campaign 051 — Final Reference Lock

**Locked direction:** Signal Arcade / Cognitive Arcade Console
**Lock date:** 2026-09-19

## Palette

- Ink navy is the primary canvas/ink, not default indigo or purple.
- Warm paper is the light canvas; paper-white is reserved for the focal panel.
- Signal coral owns the primary action and completion heat.
- Electric cyan owns active mechanic/stage cues.
- Voltage yellow owns attention/reward highlights.
- Mint owns recovery/collection/quiet positive progress.
- Domain colors remain stable across library, detail, game, charts, and results;
  saturation is concentrated on the active object rather than every surface.

## Shape and type

- Poster/block surfaces, clipped or stepped corners, small purposeful radii;
  not one giant rounded card grammar.
- Primary actions are physical console keys with a visible lower lip and
  immediate press response. Secondary actions are quiet outlined or tonal
  controls. Pills are reserved for filters/statuses, not every container.
- Display type is compact, heavy, and high-contrast. Uppercase eyebrow labels
  are sparse; body copy stays generous and readable at scale 2.
- Depth is mostly flat/bordered. Shadow is reserved for a focal hero or modal.

## Surface hierarchy

1. Home: one daily console + Today/Start or Continue.
2. Games: game worlds first, discovery controls second, no card soup.
3. Detail: hero fantasy + concise mechanic + dominant Play; analytics below.
4. GameHost: mechanic stage first, chrome recedes.
5. Results: one emotional completion object + one dominant next action.
6. Progress: calm record book with domain rails and explainable charts.
7. Profile/Rewards: intentional collection shelves and trustworthy ownership.
8. Data Management: restrained paper/ink utility surface.

## Motion and sensory boundary

Presses feel like a key: quick scale/lip response. Entrances are short and
bounded. Completion may use a finite confetti/reward beat. Reduced motion
removes travel, confetti, and looping decoration while preserving state change.
Audio/haptics continue through the existing settings-gated service only.

## Explicit rejects

No warm-beige AI editorial styling, gradient backgrounds, glass surfaces,
random neon, generic rounded dashboard grids, copy-heavy hero walls, badges as
decoration, or analytics that look like a game scoreboard.
