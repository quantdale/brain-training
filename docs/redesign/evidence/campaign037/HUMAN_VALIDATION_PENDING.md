# Campaign 037 — Human and Platform Validation Pending

Not validated in this campaign:

- independent human usability review;
- manual Android TalkBack session;
- large-text and reduced-motion settings;
- iOS/VoiceOver or physical-device behavior;
- store-signed build and system document-picker flows.

The Android emulator evidence and automated accessibility audit are recorded
separately and must not be read as substitutes for these checks.
